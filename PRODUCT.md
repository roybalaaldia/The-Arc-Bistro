# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS in three files (index.html, style.css, main.js). GSAP, ScrollTrigger and Lenis load from CDNs. No build step.

## Users

Locals of San Pablo City and nearby Laguna towns: friends, families, couples, office groups. They look the place up on a phone, check hours and photos, then call, message on Facebook or email to reserve.

## Product Purpose

One-page site for The ARC Bistro, a cafe and bistro at 208 Osmeña Street, City Subdivision, San Pablo City, Laguna. It announces the reopening and gets people to visit and reserve. Success is a booked table or a walk-in.

## Positioning

A refreshed room with Filipino, Italian, fusion and international food, pizza, coffee and a wider drinks list, open breakfast to dinner, for catch-ups, work sessions and long meals.

## Operating Context

Tuesday to Sunday 10:00 AM - 9:00 PM, Monday call ahead (hours still to be verified with the business). Reservations by phone +63 995 109 1503, Facebook message, or thearcbistro@gmail.com. Amenities: takeout, free Wi-Fi, lounge and bar, private room, parking, child-friendly, dog-friendly. Price level two pesos signs.

## Capabilities and Constraints

No online booking form exists; reservation is by phone, Facebook or email. Menu details live on Facebook. Reference design chosen by the owner: https://armanello.framer.website/ (restaurant template). Layout may be rebuilt; new sections need content from the owner. Section order and exact structure still open.

## Brand Commitments

Name "The ARC Bistro". Logo assets in assets/ (logo-light.png, logo-dark.png, favicon.png) on a forest-green ground. Tagline voice: "Everything you loved, now with more to adore." Arch is the logo's motif.

## Evidence on Hand

Real photos in assets/: hero, coffee bar, new interior, new dish, drinks, fettuccine, pizza, pasta, filipino dish, shrimp bisque, coffee, coffee and bread. No photos yet for storefront, private room, lounge details, or an ambiance background. No testimonials, awards, press or prices are on hand; do not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Make it effortless to call or message to reserve.
2. Real photos of the room and food lead; nothing invented.
3. Warm, welcoming and unhurried, not corporate.
4. Mobile first for locals checking on a phone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable contrast over photos, keyboard-usable navigation, reduced-motion respected.
